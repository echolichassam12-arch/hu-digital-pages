WageShift Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

WageShift is a wage and shift calculation application published by HU Digital. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use WageShift.

Information We Collect

WageShift does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

The current version of WageShift does not transmit your shift or wage information to HU Digital servers.

Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into WageShift, such as shift details and related calculation data, may be stored locally on your device to provide app features such as saved information and history.

This locally stored information remains on your device unless you remove it through available app functionality, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to your device's operating-system behavior.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of WageShift does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Data Sharing

HU Digital does not sell the shift or wage information you enter into WageShift. The current version does not transmit this information to HU Digital for sharing with third parties.

Children's Privacy

WageShift is a general-purpose wage calculation utility and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WageShift's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or WageShift's privacy practices, contact HU Digital using the developer contact information provided on WageShift's Google Play listing.
